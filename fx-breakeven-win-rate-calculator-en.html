Formulas

Excluding break-even trades, with an average loss of 1, an average win of RR and a win rate p:

  • Break-even win rate = 1 ÷ (1 + RR)
  • Break-even RR = (1 − win rate) ÷ win rate (the RR at which a given win rate totals zero)
  • Expectancy per trade (R) = win rate × RR − (1 − win rate)
  • Expectancy in your unit = expectancy (R) × average loss

A positive expectancy means the win rate is above the break-even win rate; a negative one means it is below.

Worked example

Suppose a month has 20 trades (no break-evens): 9 wins averaging 24 pips and 11 losses averaging 15 pips (a made-up example).

  • Win rate = 9 ÷ 20 = 45%, RR = 24 ÷ 15 = 1.6
  • Break-even win rate = 1 ÷ (1 + 1.6) ≈ 38.5% (6.5 points below the win rate)
  • Expectancy (R) = 0.45 × 1.6 − 0.55 = +0.17R
  • Expectancy (pips) = 0.17 × 15 = +2.55 pips (the same as the total, 9 × 24 − 11 × 15 = +51 pips, divided by 20 trades)
  • RR that breaks even at a 45% win rate = 0.55 ÷ 0.45 ≈ 1.22

Using it with your own records

  • Win rate and RR move together. Placing the target further away raises RR, but fewer trades may reach it, so the win rate can fall. A lower break-even win rate on paper changes nothing if the actual win rate falls further.
  • Use actual RR, not planned RR. Average win ÷ average loss from closed trades (actual RR) reflects your record better than the target ÷ stop you planned at entry.
  • Small samples swing. With 10 or 20 trades, one large win or loss moves the averages a lot.
  • Treat commissions and swaps consistently. Including or excluding them changes the average win and loss.
  • It describes past records. Expectancy is an estimate from past totals and does not predict future results.

FAQ

What is the formula for the break-even win rate?
Break-even win rate = 1 ÷ (1 + risk-reward), where risk-reward is average win ÷ average loss and break-even trades are excluded. For example, a risk-reward of 2 gives about 33.3%, and 1 gives 50%.
What does "R" mean in expectancy?
R is a unit where the average loss equals 1. An expectancy of +0.17R means that, in your record, each trade came out on average 0.17 times the average loss ahead. It is an estimate from past totals and does not predict future results.
Is anything I enter sent anywhere?
No. The calculation runs in your browser, and nothing you enter is sent or stored.
