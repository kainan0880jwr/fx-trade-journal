How it works

Log it, add it up, review it.

Motivation shows up after a win. So logging takes as few as 3 taps — even after a loss.

  1. 1Log a trade in as few as 3 taps

    Pick the pair, direction and result, then save. Pips are calculated from your entry and exit prices, and P/L from pips and lot size (for pairs quoted in your account currency, which you choose in Settings).

  2. 2Win rate, pips and PF add up for you

    Just log your trades and today’s and this month’s win rate, pips and profit factor appear on the home screen.

  3. 3Review the month

    See your progress toward the month’s goals and how your wins and losses break down, on one screen.

Privacy and security

Your trade records stay on your device.

No sign-up is needed. Trade records are encrypted on your device and are never sent to our servers.

  • The trade record database is encrypted (SQLCipher, AES-256)
  • No cloud sync. Logging and analysis work offline
  • Lock the app with Face ID or Touch ID

What the app sends

  • PRO purchases and restores (App Store, RevenueCat)
  • Checks for app updates
  • Crash and usage reports (Sentry, with a random per-device ID)

What it never sends

  • The contents of your trade records
  • Your name or email address (there is no sign-up)

Please note

  • Attached chart images are not encrypted (they are stored on your device).
  • Figures shown in widgets are not encrypted and can appear on the Lock Screen.
  • If iCloud Backup is on, your records are included in that backup.
  • Backups and CSV files exported without a password are not encrypted.

Our Privacy Policy explains what is sent and why.

FAQ

Questions before you start.

What can I use for free?
Downloading the app, logging trades, monthly stats, MT4/MT5 import, the calendar and widgets (iPhone and iPad) are free. Detailed analysis such as time of day and weekday, and watermark-free share cards, are PRO (a paid, auto-renewing subscription). Which features are free or PRO may change.
Where is my trade data stored?
Your trade records are encrypted and stored only on your device (attached chart images are stored on-device without encryption). There is no cloud sync, and logging and analysis work without an internet connection. The app connects only to purchase or restore PRO, check for app updates, and send crash and usage reports (Sentry, with a random per-device ID; never your name or trade content); the content of your trade records is never sent. If you have iCloud Backup enabled, they are also included in that backup.
What happens to my trades if I delete the app?
Your trades are stored only on your device, so deleting the app deletes them too, and we cannot restore them. Before changing phones or deleting the app, create a backup under "Data Backup" in the app's Settings. You can encrypt the backup with a password (if you forget it, the backup cannot be restored).
How do I import my MT4/MT5 history?
On your computer, right-click the Account History tab in MT4 or the History tab in MT5 and save a report (.htm in MT4, .html in MT5). Send the file to your phone with AirDrop, iCloud Drive or email, then choose it in the app under Settings → MT4/MT5 trade history import. MT4/MT5-format CSV files can be imported too. Some brokers’ formats may not be readable. Requires the latest version of the app.
Can I use it with a broker other than MT4/MT5?
Yes. Every feature works with manual entry. If your broker lets you export an MT4/MT5 report or CSV, you can also import your trades in bulk.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime?
Yes. You can cancel anytime from your device's App Store or Google Play settings. After cancelling, you'll keep PRO access until the next renewal date.
Is there an Android version?
The Android version is in closed testing on Google Play, and you can join as a tester (free to download; PRO is paid). A public release date has not been set.
Does the app give investment advice?
This app is a logging and analysis tool. It is not intended to provide investment advice or solicit any specific financial product. Past records and results do not guarantee future performance. Please make your own trading decisions at your own risk.
