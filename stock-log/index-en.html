How it works

Record, import in bulk, review by year.

Record new trades by hand, and bring in past trades from your broker's CSV.

  1. Record

    Record buys, sells, dividends and shareholder benefits for the account you choose. Stock splits and reverse splits are supported.

  2. Import in bulkPRO

    Import the trade history CSV you downloaded from your broker by matching its columns. The preview is free; confirming the import is a Stock Trade Diary PRO feature.

  3. Review by year

    Look back at the realized gain or loss and holding period of each sale, for this year, last year or all time.

Features

Record and review. Nothing more, nothing less.

Stock Trade Diary does not show market prices. It only shows the trades you actually made.

Trades, dividends and benefits together

Record buys and sells, dividends received and shareholder benefits. Stock splits and reverse splits are supported, and your average cost is recalculated automatically.

  • Japanese stocks
  • US stocks (incl. fractional)
  • Odd lots
  • ETFs and REITs

Holdings and average cost

Shares held and average cost are calculated per account and security. Japanese accounts use the total average method by default; other accounts use FIFO.

Review every sale

See the realized gain or loss and holding period of each sale, and filter by this year, last year or all time, or by profit and loss.

See your month on a calendar

Check realized gains and losses by day, and the days you recorded dividends and shareholder benefits, on a calendar.

Foreign stocks and currencies

Enter the exchange rate at the time of the trade, and the result is converted to your account currency. If your broker reported a gain or loss, that figure is shown first.

Keep your reasons and notes, find them later

Save your reason for each trade, how you felt, tags and notes. You can search your records by keyword.

Import your broker's CSV PRO

Import the trade history CSV you downloaded from your broker by matching its columns. The same trade is never imported twice, and you can undo an import.

The preview is free; confirming the import requires Stock Trade Diary PRO. Margin trades are not imported. Some broker formats may not be readable.

Screens from the actual app. Security names and figures are made-up sample data, not real results.

Stock Trade Diary is for recording and reviewing your own trades. It does not provide investment advice or tell you which securities to buy or sell. Figures are calculated from the records you enter or import; for tax filing, please check the documents from your broker.

Privacy and security

Your trade records are not sent to our servers.

There is no account to create. Your records are encrypted and stored on your device, and are not sent to our servers (iPhone backups include your encrypted records).

  • Your record database is encrypted (SQLCipher)
  • Your broker login ID and password are never asked for or stored
  • Lock the app with Face ID, fingerprint or passcode. Contents are hidden in the app switcher too
  • Move to a new phone with a password-protected encrypted backup
  • Error reports are designed not to include your records, such as securities, amounts or notes

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is there an app to record stock trades and dividends together?

Stock Trade Diary is an iPhone and Android app that records stock trades, dividends and shareholder benefits in one place. It supports Japanese and US stocks, and lets you review your realized gains and received dividends year by year.

Can I record shareholder benefits (perks)?

Yes. You can record what you received and your own estimate of its value, and benefits are totaled by year alongside your dividends.

Does it connect to my brokerage account?

No. It never uses your broker login ID or password. You record trades by hand or import a CSV you downloaded from your broker.

Can I keep accounts at several brokers in one app?

Yes. Register an account for each broker and account type (such as taxable or tax-free), and record them all in one app. Holdings and average cost are calculated per account.

Where is my data stored?

Your records are encrypted and stored on your device, and are not sent to our servers. iPhone device backups (such as iCloud) include your encrypted records. There is no account to create.

What can I use for free?

Recording trades, dividends, shareholder benefits and splits, holdings and average cost, reviewing realized gains, the calendar, app lock, encrypted backup and CSV export are free. Confirming a broker CSV import, summaries by reason and tag, the monthly review and other extras are Stock Trade Diary PRO features (¥500 per month or ¥3,980 per year, tax-included prices on the Japanese App Store).

Does it support US stocks and foreign withholding tax?

Yes. You can record US stocks, including fractional shares, and enter dividends as gross, foreign tax, domestic tax and net. Enter the exchange rate at the time of the trade, and the result is converted to your account currency.

Can I record tax-free accounts?

Yes. Register each account as taxable or tax-free (or, for Japanese brokers, specific, general or NISA). Average cost is calculated separately for each account.

Which brokers' CSV files can I import?

Because you match the columns yourself, CSV files from many brokers may work. Some formats cannot be read, so please check the preview before importing.

Can I see stock prices or unrealized gains?

No. Stock Trade Diary does not fetch market data such as stock prices. It focuses on reviewing the trades you made: realized gains, dividends and benefits.

Does it calculate taxes?

Tax on sales is not calculated automatically: withholding is often netted over the year, so per-trade estimates may not match. For dividends, you can fill in a tax estimate based on general rates, but please correct it to the amount on your broker's report.

What happens to my records when I change phones?

Create a password-protected encrypted backup in Settings and restore it on your new phone. If you forget the password, the backup cannot be restored, so keep it safe.

How are my records backed up?

In Settings, you can create a password-protected encrypted backup file and choose where to save it. Your iPhone's device backups (such as iCloud) also include your encrypted records. On Android, records are not included in device backups, so use the encrypted backup in Settings when you change phones.

How do I cancel Stock Trade Diary PRO?

On iPhone, go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On Android, go to Payments & subscriptions in Google Play. If you cancel before the end of the period (at least 24 hours before on the App Store), you are not charged for the next period. Your records are not deleted, and the free features keep working.

Which devices and languages are supported?

Stock Trade Diary is an app for iPhone and Android. It can be shown in 11 languages: Japanese, English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Indonesian, Turkish, Hindi and Vietnamese.
