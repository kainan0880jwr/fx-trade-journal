Features

Record and review. Nothing more, nothing less.

Stock Log does not show market prices. It only shows the trades you actually made.

✎

Trades, dividends and benefits together

Record buys and sells, dividends received and shareholder benefits. Stock splits and reverse splits are supported, and your average cost is recalculated automatically.

Holdings and average cost

Shares held and average cost are calculated per account and security. Japanese accounts use the total average method by default; other accounts use FIFO.

↺

Review every sale

See the realized gain or loss and holding period of each sale, and filter by this year, last year or all time, or by profit and loss.

¥$

Foreign stocks and currencies

Enter the exchange rate at the time of the trade, and the result is converted to your account currency. If your broker reported a gain or loss, that figure is shown first.

⇪

Import your broker's CSV PRO

Import the trade history CSV you downloaded from your broker by matching its columns. The same trade is never imported twice, and you can undo an import.

  1. Download your trade history as a CSV from your broker's website
  2. Choose the CSV from Files on your phone
  3. Match the columns, check the preview, and import

The preview is free; confirming the import requires PRO. Margin trades are not imported. Some broker formats may not be readable.

Stock Log is for recording and reviewing your own trades. It does not provide investment advice or tell you which securities to buy or sell. Figures are calculated from the records you enter or import; for tax filing, please check the documents from your broker.

Privacy and security

Your trades stay on your device.

There is no account to create. Your records are encrypted on your device and are never sent to our servers.

  • Your record database is encrypted (SQLCipher)
  • Your broker login ID and password are never asked for or stored
  • Lock the app with Face ID, fingerprint or passcode. Contents are hidden in the app switcher too
  • Move to a new phone with a password-protected encrypted backup
  • Error reports never include your records, such as securities, amounts or notes

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I see stock prices or unrealized gains?

No. Stock Log does not fetch market data such as stock prices. It focuses on reviewing the trades you made: realized gains, dividends and benefits.

Does it connect to my brokerage account?

No. It never uses your broker login ID or password. You record trades by hand or import a CSV you downloaded from your broker.

Does it calculate taxes?

No. Tax rules depend on your country and account type, and per-trade estimates often differ from what is actually withheld. You can record pre-tax gains and net dividends.

What happens to my records when I change phones?

Create a password-protected encrypted backup in Settings and restore it on your new phone. If you forget the password, the backup cannot be restored, so keep it safe.

Can I record tax-free accounts?

Yes. Register each account as taxable or tax-free (or, for Japanese brokers, specific, general or NISA). Average cost is calculated separately for each account.

Which brokers' CSV files can I import?

Because you match the columns yourself, CSV files from many brokers may work. Some formats cannot be read, so please check the preview before importing.
