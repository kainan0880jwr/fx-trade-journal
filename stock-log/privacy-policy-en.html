Privacy Policy

This page is a translation of the Japanese original (プライバシーポリシー). If there is any difference between the two, the Japanese version prevails.

This policy explains how the app "Stock Log" (Japanese name: かぶログ; the "App") handles information.
Effective date: November 15, 2026

1. Business operator

池端大輝 (sole proprietor, Japan). The address will be provided without delay upon request. See section 14 for contact details.

2. Where your records are stored

Records you enter or import into the App (accounts, securities, trades, dividends, shareholder benefits, reasons, notes and so on) are stored only on your device and encrypted on the device. There is no operator server, and the operator never receives or views your records.

On iPhone, device backups (such as iCloud Backup) include your encrypted records and their encryption key, so that you can carry your records over when you change devices. Please keep your Apple ID and password safe.

3. Information the App does not collect

The App does not collect your name, email address, brokerage ID or password, or account numbers. There is no account registration. The App does not measure usage for advertising or to track you across other apps or websites. It also does not communicate to obtain market data such as stock prices.

4. Sending to third-party services (information provided under Japan's external transmission rules of the Telecommunications Business Act)

  • Sentry (Functional Software, Inc., USA): To investigate app problems, only when an error occurs, the App sends the type and location of the error (stack trace), the history of operations just before the error (such as screen changes; record contents are not included), the device and OS type, and the app version. The App is designed not to send the contents of your records (securities, amounts, notes and so on). Your IP address reaches Sentry when the data is sent. Sent data is deleted according to Sentry's retention settings.
  • RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA): To check the status of the paid plan and to count purchases, the App sends purchase information (the store receipt) and a random identifier not linked to your name or similar information, when the App starts and when you make a purchase. Your IP address reaches RevenueCat when the data is sent.
  • Apple (App Store) / Google (Google Play): Purchases and payments for the paid plan are made through each store, and each company's privacy policy applies.

The recipients above are companies in the United States. Information about the personal information protection system of the United States is available on the website of Japan's Personal Information Protection Commission. For each company's practices, please see its privacy policy.

5. Information received through inquiries

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the content of your inquiry. We use them only to reply to you and to keep a record of the inquiry, and we do not provide them to third parties (except where required by law).

6. Reading trade history files

When importing a CSV, the App reads the file you choose on your device and saves it as records. The file itself and its name are not saved, and the temporary file on the device is deleted after reading. The file contents are never sent outside the device.

7. Biometric authentication (App Lock)

App Lock uses your device's Face ID, fingerprint or passcode. Biometric data is managed by the device's operating system, and the App never receives it.

8. Backups and exports

An encrypted backup is created as a file encrypted with a password you choose, and you choose where to save it. The password is not stored. Files created by CSV export are not encrypted. You are responsible for managing them where you save them.

9. Deleting data

Because your records exist only on your device, deleting the App also deletes your records (unless restored from a device backup). The operator holds no records, so there is no need to ask the operator to delete them.

10. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13.

11. Requests and complaints

Questions about this policy, requests to notify the purpose of use of, disclose, correct or stop using retained personal data, and complaints are accepted at the contact in section 14. After confirming your identity, we will respond without delay. Because the operator does not hold your records, you can view and delete the records themselves on your own device.

12. Security measures

We encrypt records on the device, minimize what is sent outside (section 4), remove record contents from error reports, and limit access to the inquiry mailbox.

13. For users outside Japan

The operator of the App (the data controller) is a sole proprietor in Japan. As described in section 4, information for error reports and purchase checks is sent to companies in the United States. To exercise rights under the laws of your place of residence (such as access, correction, deletion or objection), please contact us at the address in section 14.

15. Disclaimer

The App is for recording and reviewing your own trades and does not provide investment advice. Figures shown are calculated mechanically from the records you entered or imported and may not match your broker's figures. For tax filings and similar purposes, please check your broker's trade reports and other official documents.

16. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will announce the new content and its effective date on this page and in the App.
