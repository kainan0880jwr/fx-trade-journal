Privacy Policy

This page is a translation of the Japanese original (プライバシーポリシー). If there is any difference between the two, the Japanese version prevails.

This policy explains how the app "Stock Log" (Japanese name: かぶログ; the "App") handles information.
Effective date: November 15, 2026

1. Where your records are stored

Records you enter or import into the App (accounts, securities, trades, dividends, shareholder benefits, reasons, notes, etc.) are stored only on your device and are encrypted on the device. The operator has no server, and the operator never receives or views your records.

2. Information we do not collect

The App does not receive your name, email address, broker login ID or password, or account numbers. There is no account registration. The App does not measure your behavior for advertising or analytics, and it does not connect to the internet to get market data such as stock prices.

3. Information sent to third-party services

  • Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.): To investigate bugs, when an error occurs the App sends the type and location of the error, the device and OS type, and the app version. The contents of your records (securities, amounts, notes, etc.) are not sent.
  • RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.): To check the status of the paid plan, the App sends purchase information and an anonymous identifier created for each device.
  • Apple (App Store) / Google (Google Play): Purchases and payments for the paid plan are made through each store, and each company's privacy policy applies.

4. Importing trade history files

When you import a CSV, the App reads the file you choose on your device and saves it as records. The App does not save the file itself or its name, and deletes the temporary file on the device after reading. The contents of the file are never sent outside the device.

5. Biometric authentication (app lock)

The app lock uses your device's Face ID, fingerprint or passcode. Biometric information is managed by the device's operating system, and the App never receives it.

6. Backups and exports

An encrypted backup is created as a file encrypted with a password you choose, and you choose where to save it. The password is not stored. Files created by CSV export are not encrypted. You are responsible for managing the files where you save them.

7. Deleting data

Because your records exist only on your device, deleting the App also deletes your records (unless they are restored from a device backup). The operator holds no records, so there is no need to ask the operator to delete them.

8. Children's privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13.

9. Requests about personal data

The operator does not hold your records, but questions and requests about this policy are accepted at the contact below.

10. Security measures

The App encrypts records on the device, keeps data sent outside the device to a minimum (section 3), and removes record contents from error reports.

11. Disclaimer

The App is for recording and reviewing your own trades and does not provide investment advice. Figures shown are calculated from the records you enter or import, and their accuracy is not guaranteed. For tax filing and similar purposes, please check the trade reports and other documents from your broker.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the change will be announced on this page.
