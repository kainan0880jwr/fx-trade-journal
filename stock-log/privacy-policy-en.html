Privacy Policy

This page is a translation of the Japanese original (プライバシーポリシー). If there is any difference between the two, the Japanese version prevails.

This policy explains how the app "Stock Trade Diary" (Japanese name: 株トレードログ; the "App") handles information.
Effective date: November 15, 2026
Last revised: November 25, 2026

1. Business operator

池端大輝 (sole proprietor, Japan). The address will be provided without delay upon request. See section 14 for contact details.

2. Where your records are stored

Records you enter or import into the App (accounts, securities, trades, dividends, shareholder benefits, reasons, notes and so on) are stored only on your device and encrypted on the device. There is no operator server, and the operator never receives your records (except when you send them yourself in an inquiry).

On iPhone, device backups (such as iCloud Backup) include your encrypted records. Depending on the backup method and the device you restore to, the encryption key may not be carried over. Before changing devices, please create an encrypted backup in the App (section 8).

If you use the home screen or lock screen widgets, the App stores in a widget area on your device the counts needed for display (trades this month and sells without a review) and, only when you turn it on in settings, this year's totals of dividends received and realized profit and loss. This area is not covered by the encryption of your records and relies on the protection of your device's operating system. Stock names and individual records are not stored there. Widgets and notifications are shown outside the app lock.

3. Information the App does not collect

The App does not collect your name, email address, brokerage ID or password, or account numbers. There is no account registration. The App does not measure usage for advertising or to track you across other apps or websites. It also does not communicate to obtain market data such as stock prices.

The App does not sell personal information or share it for targeted (behavioral) advertising. The App does not respond to browser Do Not Track signals, but it does not perform any tracking measurement.

4. Sending to third-party services (information provided under Japan's external transmission rules of the Telecommunications Business Act)

  • Sentry (Functional Software, Inc., USA): To investigate app problems, only when an error occurs, the App sends the type and location of the error (stack trace), the history of operations just before the error (such as screen changes; record contents are not included), the device and OS type, and the app version. The App is designed not to send the contents of your records (securities, amounts, notes and so on). Your IP address reaches Sentry when the data is sent. Sent data is deleted according to Sentry's retention settings.You can stop error reports at any time with "Send error reports" in the App's settings. On devices whose region is set to India or Brazil, error reports are not sent until you turn them on.
  • RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA): To check the status of the paid plan and to count purchases, the App sends purchase information (the store receipt), a random identifier not linked to your name or similar information, the device and OS type, the app version, and your language and store country or region, when the App starts and when you make a purchase. On iPhone, an identifier assigned to the device for each app developer (IDFV; not an advertising identifier) is also sent. Your IP address reaches RevenueCat when the data is sent.
  • Apple (App Store) / Google (Google Play): Purchases and payments for the paid plan are made through each store, and each company's privacy policy applies.

The recipients above are companies in the United States. The operator has ensured, with each company, a system that meets the standards set by Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information (equivalent measures). Upon request to the contact in section 14, we will provide information about these measures without delay, including how the system was put in place, an outline of the measures each company takes, how and how often their implementation is checked, the name of the destination country (the United States), and any system in that country that may affect the implementation of the measures. Information about the personal information protection system of the United States is available on the website of Japan's Personal Information Protection Commission. For each company's practices, please see its privacy policy.

5. Information received through inquiries

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the content of your inquiry. We use them only to reply to you and to keep a record of the inquiry, and we delete them three years after we finish replying. We do not provide them to third parties (except where required by law).

6. Reading trade history files

When importing a CSV, the App reads the file you choose on your device and saves it as records. The file itself and its name are not saved, and the App is designed to delete the temporary file on the device after reading. The file contents are never sent outside the device.

7. Biometric authentication (App Lock)

App Lock uses your device's Face ID, fingerprint or passcode. Biometric data is managed by the device's operating system, and the App never receives it.

8. Backups and exports

An encrypted backup is created as a file encrypted with a password you choose, and you choose where to save it. The password is not stored. Files created by CSV export are not encrypted. You are responsible for managing them where you save them.

When you restore from a backup and your records are replaced, the App keeps one encrypted copy of the records from before the replacement on your device, so that the replacement can be undone. The copy is deleted 30 days after it was made, when it is replaced by a newer copy, or when you tap "Delete this copy". The copy is never sent off your device.

9. Deleting data

Because your records exist only on your device, deleting the App also deletes your records (unless restored from a device backup). The operator holds no records, so there is no need to ask the operator to delete them.

10. Children's privacy

The App is intended for people aged 18 or older and is not directed at anyone under 18.

11. Requests and complaints

Questions about this policy, requests to notify the purpose of use of, disclose, correct or stop using retained personal data, and complaints are accepted at the contact in section 14. After confirming your identity, we will respond without delay. Because the operator does not hold your records, you can view and delete the records themselves on your own device.

12. Security measures

We encrypt records on the device, minimize what is sent outside (section 4), remove record contents from error reports, and limit access to the inquiry mailbox.

13. For users outside Japan

The operator of the App (the data controller) is a sole proprietor in Japan. As described in section 4, information for error reports and purchase checks is sent to companies in the United States. To exercise rights under the laws of your place of residence (such as access, correction, deletion or objection), please contact us at the address in section 14.

13-2. For users in Switzerland and similar regions

If you live in a region where the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection or similar laws apply, such as Switzerland, please note the following.

  • Legal bases for processing: Sending data to check your purchase status (RevenueCat, section 4) is done to perform the paid plan contract. Error reports (Sentry, section 4) are sent for the operator's legitimate interest in fixing problems in the App. Inquiry emails (section 5) are handled for the operator's legitimate interest in replying to your inquiry.
  • Retention periods: Error reports are deleted according to Sentry's retention settings. Purchase information is kept for as long as needed to check purchases. Inquiry emails are handled as described in section 5.
  • Transfers to the United States: The recipients in section 4 are companies in the United States. Transfers to the United States are based on each company's participation in the Data Privacy Framework (including the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework) or on standard contractual clauses.
  • Your rights: Requests to exercise rights such as access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, objection and data portability are accepted at the contact in section 14. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority of your country or region.
