★ Description (4000 characters max)

Make your own trading visible

Whether you are new to forex or have traded for years, a trading journal makes
visible how you actually trade. FX Trade Journal is a serious trade-tracking app
for traders who want to learn from their own history, with direct MT4/MT5 trade
history import.

■ Log

・Log a trade in one tap: pair, result, pips, lot size and P&L
・Attach chart screenshots to any trade
・Reflection notes and tags to keep things organised
・Bulk-import MT4/MT5 trade history CSV
・Register your own rules and tick them off as you log

■ Analyse

・Monthly dashboard calculates win rate, pips, profit factor and P&L automatically
・Breakdowns by currency pair and by trading style
・Calendar view colour-codes each day by result
・Daily streak tracking keeps the habit alive

■ Set goals

・Set goals for each day, week, month and year
・Choose from rule adherence, pips, P&L or win rate
・Days where you meet every goal you set are outlined on the calendar
・Days with no trades are never marked, so a day off is not counted as a win

■ Home Screen widgets

・See this month's win rate and total pips at a glance
・The medium size also shows profit factor, trade count and your logging streak
・Lock Screen widgets included
・Tap any widget to jump straight to the trade entry screen

■ Share

・Turn your monthly results into a clean, shareable stats card
・PRO removes the watermark

■ Who it is for

・Beginners who want to build a logging habit
・MT4/MT5 users tired of tracking trades in a spreadsheet
・Anyone who wants a proper monthly read on win rate and pips
・Traders who like sharing their results
・Anyone who wants to keep a trading journal entirely on their phone

■ FX Trade Journal PRO

A monthly or yearly subscription unlocks the premium tier:

・Weekly view and in-depth insight analysis
・Time-of-day and weekday breakdowns
・Tag analysis, risk-reward analysis, equity curve and mental tracking
・Achievement badges (33 of them)
・Yearly tab and position size calculator
・Stats cards without the watermark
・All future premium features

The price is shown on the purchase confirmation screen.
Subscriptions can be cancelled at any time from your device's Settings app.

■ About your data

・All data is stored locally on your device
・Logging and analysis work offline. An internet connection is required only to
  purchase or restore PRO
・Trade records are stored encrypted
・Built privacy-first

■ Supported brokers and platforms

Any broker that lets you export trade history as MT4/MT5-format CSV works with
the app. Manual entry is fully supported for every broker and platform.

■ Please note

This app is a tool for recording and reviewing your own trades. It does not
provide investment advice or solicit any specific financial product. Past
records do not guarantee future results. Investment decisions are your own
responsibility.
