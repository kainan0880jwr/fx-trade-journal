How to Import MT4/MT5 Trade History to iPhone
Save the Report, Send It, Import It

Published: November 18, 2026 Reading time: 6 min

This article explains how to import MT4/MT5 trade history into an app. It is not investment advice or a recommendation to buy or sell anything. Keeping and reviewing records does not guarantee profits, and all investment decisions are your own responsibility. Menu names in MT4/MT5 can differ slightly by version and language.

Re-typing past trades into a journal one by one is tedious. If you trade with MT4 or MT5, you can save your history to a file and import it in one go. This guide walks through saving your history in the desktop versions of MT4 and MT5, sending the file to your iPhone, and importing it into FX Trade Journal — and what to check if something goes wrong.

Before you start

  • Save the file on your computer. The MT4 and MT5 mobile apps cannot save your trade history to a file.
  • Save it as a report (HTML). MT4 gives you an .htm file and MT5 an .html file. CSV trade histories from your broker's website work too.
  • PDF and Excel (.xlsx) files can't be read. If MT5 offers Excel or PDF, choose HTML instead.
  • Importing is free. The file is read on your iPhone only, and your trades are not sent anywhere.

Saving your history in MT4 (desktop)

  1. Open the “Account History” tab in the Terminal window.
  2. Right-click the list and choose “All History” (or just the period you want).
  3. Right-click again and choose “Save as Report”. You get a file like Statement.htm.

“Save as Report” gives a smaller file than “Save as Detailed Report”.

Saving your history in MT5 (desktop)

  1. Open the “History” tab in the Toolbox.
  2. Right-click the list and choose “Report” → “HTML”.
  3. You get a file like ReportHistory-….html.

The “Open XML (Excel)” option in the same menu, and PDFs saved from the chart-style report window, can't be read. Save as HTML from the right-click menu of the History tab.

Sending the file to your iPhone

Send the file to yourself with one of these and keep it somewhere the Files app can open.

  • AirDrop (from a Mac) — save it to the Files app when it arrives.
  • iCloud Drive or Google Drive — drop it in a folder on your computer and open it from the Files app on your iPhone.
  • Email — attach it to an email to yourself and save the attachment to the Files app.

Importing it into the app

  1. In FX Trade Journal, open Settings and tap “MT4/MT5 Trade History Import” (on first launch you can also choose “Import MT4/MT5 history” on the welcome screen).
  2. Choose the file you sent.
  3. The app shows how many trades were imported. Closed trades are added with pair, direction, lot size, prices and P/L.

Importing the same file twice won't create duplicates — trades already imported are skipped. If you import the wrong file, “Undo last import” in Settings removes it. If you use more than one account, the app uses the account number in the report to pick the account (a second account and beyond is PRO).

If it doesn't work

Message or symptomCommon causeWhat to do
“Please select a CSV or TXT file, or an MT4/MT5 report (HTML)”A PDF, Excel file or image was chosenSave an HTML report from MT4/MT5 again
“No closed-trade table was found in this report”The report only has open positions or ordersSave from Account History (MT4) or the History tab (MT5)
“Could not read the file encoding”The CSV was saved in an encoding other than UTF-8In Excel, save as “CSV UTF-8”
Trade times are off by a few hoursMT4/MT5 times are your broker's server timeSet “Import time adjustment” in Settings to “Auto (follows DST)” or a fixed number of hours
Fewer trades than expectedOpen positions are not importedImport again after closing them (trades already imported are skipped)

Each import can be up to 10 MB and 20,000 trades. For larger histories, save the report in shorter periods.

FAQ

Can I import trade history from the MT4/MT5 mobile app?
The MT4 and MT5 mobile apps cannot save trade history to a file, so use an HTML report saved from the desktop version, or a CSV from your broker's website.
Is importing free?
Yes. MT4/MT5 import is available on the free plan. Importing into a second account or beyond, and detailed analysis such as by session and weekday, are PRO (paid, auto-renewing).
Is my trade data sent anywhere?
The file is read and saved on your iPhone only, and your trades are not sent to our servers. Records are stored encrypted on your device.
Is there an Android version?
The Android version is in closed testing on Google Play. For now the app runs on iPhone and iPad (iOS 16.4 or later).
