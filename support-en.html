Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Where is my recorded data stored?
A. Your trade records and chart images are stored locally on your device and are never sent to an external server. When switching devices, use the JSON backup feature in Settings to export your data, then import it on your new device. Note that crash reports and limited usage information are sent to third-party services to detect bugs and process in-app purchases — see our Privacy Policy for details.

Q. How do I cancel my subscription?
A. Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → FX Trade Journal.

Q. My purchase isn't showing up.
A. Try the "Restore Purchases" button in the App. If the issue persists, please contact us with details about your device and OS version.

Q. Can I import MT4/MT5 trade history?
A. Yes — go to Settings to import an MT4-format CSV file. Formats vary between brokers, so some files may not import correctly.
